05 // About

A bit about me

TB

I'm a mechanical engineering senior at San Diego State, graduating in May 2027. I'm happiest where design meets the shop floor: drawing a part, proving it with numbers, and then standing at the machine while it gets made.

Leading teams has taught me as much as any class. Running a five-person capstone and leading a 30-person humanitarian project means keeping scope, schedule, and budget honest, and writing documents that a professional engineer will actually sign off on. My internship at Grifols showed me how a regulated plant keeps its equipment running, and why clean documentation matters as much as clean design.

After graduation I'm moving to Dallas–Fort Worth, looking for a role in mechanical design, manufacturing, or process engineering where I can keep learning fast and own real hardware.
